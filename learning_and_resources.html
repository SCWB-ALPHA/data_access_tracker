Learning & Resources

Quick, practical guidance for responsible data use—built for busy teams and inclusive audiences.

Compliance Do & Don’t

  • Do request access for a specific purpose
  • Do use the least data needed
  • Do keep data where it is approved to live
  • Do log your access and exports
  • Do report any incident immediately
  • Don’t share raw data in chat apps
  • Don’t email files without approval
  • Don’t store data on personal devices
  • Don’t access beyond your role
  • Don’t keep personal copies

Key Concepts (plain language)

  • Purpose limitation: use data only for the reason approved.
  • Least privilege: only what you need, nothing more.
  • PII: information that identifies a person (name, email, ID).
  • Audit trail: a record of who accessed what and when.

Practice: your 60-second checklist

  1. Is my purpose clear and approved?
  2. Am I using the minimum dataset?
  3. Am I saving it only in approved locations?
  4. Will my action auto-log in the system?
  5. Do I need to notify anyone (e.g., export)?

If something goes wrong

  • Stop and contain (disconnect, delete local copy)
  • Document what happened (no blame, just facts)
  • Notify an Admin immediately via the approved channel
