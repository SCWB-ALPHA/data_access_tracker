What’s already in

In progress

  • Glossary for technical terms used in data governance
  • Screen reader label audit (ARIA landmarks, form labels)

Deaf-friendly tips we follow

  • Keep sentences short; avoid jargon or explain it simply
  • Use consistent button words: “Request”, “Approve”, “Export”
  • Prefer visual steps and checklists over long paragraphs
  • Provide confirmations after actions (submitted, approved, denied)
